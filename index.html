⚡ WHAT WILL KILL THIS PLAN

  • Pausing SIPs during baby year "just 3 months" — they never restart at the same level.
  • Child insurance plans / child ULIPs post-birth. Every agent will call. Say no every time.
  • Lifestyle inflation: 15% raise → 14% more spending = zero progress. Rule: 80% of every hike goes to investments first.
  • Skipping the April SIP step-up for even 3 years = ₹1 Cr+ corpus loss. Set a phone reminder right now.
  • No health insurance — one uninsured hospitalization = 6 months of savings wiped.
  • Buying car without AMD lease tax advantage = costs ₹4–5L extra over 4 years vs loan.
