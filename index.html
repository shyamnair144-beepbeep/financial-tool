SIP Power: Rupee Cost Averaging

BULL MARKET
SIP buys fewer units at higher NAV. Your wealth grows rapidly as market rises. Step-up SIPs amplify this effect. Long-term winners always buy throughout market cycles.
BEAR MARKET
SIP buys MORE units at lower NAV. Average cost per unit falls. Recovery amplifies gains. KEY: Never stop SIPs during crash - this is where long-term wealth is built.
