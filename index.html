LTCG Annual Harvest Calendar

Tax Harvesting Strategy: Every January, sell equity MF units to realize exactly ₹1.25L in gains per person (₹2.5L for the couple) — then immediately reinvest. This books gains tax-free under the annual LTCG exemption, resetting your cost basis. Over 28 years, this saves an estimated ₹12–18L in taxes.
HOW MUCH TO SELL EACH JANUARY
Formula: Amount to sell = ₹1.25L ÷ Gain% on that fund
Example: Fund has ₹5L corpus, grew 25% from ₹4L invested → gain% = 20%
→ Sell ₹1.25L ÷ 20% = ₹6.25L worth of units to realize ₹1.25L gain
→ Immediately reinvest ₹6.25L in the same fund at new NAV
→ Cost basis resets from ₹5L → ₹6.25L. Future gains are smaller.
✅ LTCG @ 12.5% above ₹1.25L exemption (Budget 2024). Debt MFs taxed at slab rate — no harvest benefit for debt funds.

Formula: Gain% = (Current Value − Invested) ÷ Invested
Units to sell = ₹1,25,000 ÷ Gain%
Example: Nifty 50 SIP value ₹8L, invested ₹6L → Gain = ₹2L, Gain% = 33% → Sell ₹1,25,000 ÷ 33% = ₹3,78,787 worth of units

4
Immediately reinvest the SAME amount in the SAME fund
In Kuvera: Transact → Invest → Lump Sum → Same fund → ₹3,78,787 → Confirm.
This resets your cost basis to the new (higher) NAV. Future gains on these units will be smaller, meaning less future tax.
⚠️ Do NOT wait — reinvest the same day or next day. NAV difference is irrelevant. The goal is cost basis reset.
5
Repeat for wife's account (separate ₹1.25L exemption)
