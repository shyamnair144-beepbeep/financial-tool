## 🔄 WHEN TO REPLACE A FUND

### Replace ONLY if:

| Reason | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Underperformance** | 2 consecutive years of 5%+ underperformance vs benchmark | Replace |
| **Manager Left** | Star manager quits, new manager is rookie (<3 years experience) | Replace |
| **Expense Ratio Spike** | ER increases by >0.20% without justification | Switch to cheaper alternative |
| **SEBI Ban** | Fund manager banned, penalties imposed | Replace immediately |
| **Strategy Change** | Fund changes from value → momentum or large cap → mid cap | Evaluate, likely replace |
| **AUM Explosion** | Small cap fund AUM goes from ₹5K Cr to ₹50K Cr in 1 year | Replace (size will hurt returns) |

### DON'T Replace if:

| Reason | Why Not | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **1 year underperformance** | Too short to judge | WATCH for 1 more year |
| **Market crash (-30%)** | All funds will fall | HOLD, increase SIP |
| **Negative 1Q/6M returns** | Short-term noise | IGNORE completely |
| **Friend recommended new fund** | Chasing performance | STICK to your plan |
| **New fund with great 1Y return** | Survivorship bias | IGNORE (not proven over 3+ years) |
